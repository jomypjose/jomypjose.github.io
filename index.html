02 Full-stack application · 2025

AssetPulse

Turning scattered IT inventory into one connected workspace. An asset intelligence platform for tracking hardware and software across 440+ nationwide branches.

  • Node.js
  • Asset management
  • Enterprise software
Explore the project

The challenge

Managing hardware and software across a nationwide branch network requires a consistent, centralized view of IT assets.

My contribution

Built and launched AssetPulse while serving as Information Technology Officer at ESAF Cooperative. I brought first-hand experience of branch IT operations into a Node.js application for centralized hardware and software tracking.

Scope & presentation

The platform covers hardware and software inventory across a nationwide branch network. The visual is an interface concept; it illustrates the scope of the work rather than a production screenshot.

The outcome

Centralized hardware and software tracking for 440+ branches, making asset information easier to organize and access.
