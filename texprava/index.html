THE WORKSPACE

From a quick note
to a finished document.

01 / REAL DOCUMENTS

Keep the structure.

  • Open, edit and save Word (.docx), OpenDocument (.odt), RTF and HTML files.
  • Word files keep everything you don’t edit – headers, footers, footnotes, comments, tracked changes – and you can read their notes and comments.
  • Headings, bulleted, numbered and nested lists, checklists, tables, pictures and links.
  • Fonts, sizes, colours, highlight, alignment, indentation and line spacing.
  • Export to PDF, print, or share as a message, Word document or PDF.
  • Templates for letters, résumés, meeting notes and to-do lists.
02 / FIND YOUR WORK

Pick up where you left off.

  • A library of open, pinned and recent documents, searchable by name and text.
  • Outline: jump to any heading in a long document.
  • Tap a document’s name to see where it is kept and when it was last saved.
03 / HANDWRITING

Let your hand lead.

  • Write or sketch with a stylus or your finger on a ruled page.
  • Pen, highlighter and eraser; stroke width follows pen pressure.
  • Touch the page with a stylus and handwriting starts automatically.
  • Palm rejection, so a resting hand doesn’t draw.
  • Resize pictures and drawings by dragging or pinching.
04 / VOICE

Give your ideas a voice.

  • Dictate notes in English and 22 Indian languages, plus French, Spanish and Portuguese.
  • Spoken punctuation in Indian languages – say “पूर्ण विराम” or “പുതിയ വരി”.
  • Read aloud with highlighting, in the language of each sentence.
05 / YOUR LANGUAGE

Feel at home.

  • Menus in English, മലയാളം, हिन्दी, தமிழ், ಕನ್ನಡ, తెలుగు, Français, Español and Português.
06 / SAFE BY DESIGN

Keep your work safe.

  • Drafts are kept on your phone and restored after a restart.
  • Undo that survives closing the app.
  • Version history: see and restore earlier versions.
  • Lock private notes with your fingerprint, face or PIN – stored encrypted.
  • Warns you when another app changed a file you have open.
  • Optional autosave.
07 / PRIVATE

Your words stay yours.

  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Texprava has no internet access; your writing stays on your phone unless you share it.
08 / SCAN TEXT

From paper to page.

  • Photograph a printed page, or choose a picture, and its text is typed into your document.
  • Works offline, on your phone, in English and all 22 Indian languages.
09 / TEXT AND CODE

Room for code, too.

  • Plain text, Markdown, JSON, XML and 30 more file types, each with its own syntax colours.
  • Markdown and HTML preview, find and replace with regular expressions, go to line.

BEHIND THE APP

Protect the work.
Then get out
of the way.

I built Texprava in Kotlin, with document handling and recovery designed around the files people trust it with.

Keep format logic testable

Format readers and writers use plain Kotlin so document conversions can be tested on the JVM. The project includes unit tests for format round trips, text encoding, editor behaviour and version history.

Protect the original

When a document contains unsupported content, the editor warns the user and asks for a new filename instead of overwriting the original. Before saving, it checks for changes made by another app.

Recover between sessions

Atomic local drafts, persistent undo and version history help protect work across app restarts. Android’s file picker gives users control over where files are opened and saved.

Keep privacy explicit

Locked-note contents are encrypted on the device. No account, ads or tracking; optional speech services and user-selected cloud storage operate under their own policies.
