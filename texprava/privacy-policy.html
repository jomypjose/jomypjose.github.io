Texprava

More than an editor

Privacy policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Texprava is a document, note and handwriting editor for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: Texprava does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data.

No account, no internet access

Texprava does not ask you to sign in and does not have permission to access the internet. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code.

Your documents

Documents, drafts, version history and undo history are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, or in the files and locations you choose when you open or save a document (for example your phone's storage or a cloud folder you pick in Android's file picker). Texprava does not send your documents anywhere. Files you share are sent only to the app you choose.

Locked notes are encrypted on your device with a key kept in the Android Keystore and are opened with your phone's screen lock. Texprava never sees your fingerprint, face data, PIN or password; Android checks them.

Projects, tags, bookmarks, folded outline sections and your personal dictionary are kept in the app's private settings on your device. A bookmark in a locked document keeps only its position, not any of its text.

Searching your saved files

Search saved files reads only the folders you choose in Android's folder picker, which gives Texprava access to that folder alone. The text of supported files in it is stored in an index in the app's private storage, encrypted with a key kept in the Android Keystore. Locked documents are left out. You can update or remove each folder; removing a folder, or the whole index, deletes its search text and gives back Texprava's access to the folder. If you turn on reading scanned PDFs, their pages are read on your device by the same OCR engine as Scan text; nothing is sent anywhere.

Spell checking

Spell checking is off until you turn it on for a language. When you run Check spelling, the words being checked are passed to your phone's own spell checker, which is part of your keyboard app (for example Samsung Keyboard or Gboard), on your device. Texprava itself has no internet access; how that app handles the words is up to it, under its own privacy policy. Nothing in your document is changed unless you choose a suggestion.

Microphone and dictation

The microphone permission is used only while you dictate. Speech recognition is performed by the speech service installed on your phone (for example Google's speech services). Texprava asks that service to recognise speech on the device when an offline language pack is available; otherwise the service may process your audio online under its own privacy policy. Texprava itself does not record, store or transmit audio.

The dictation transcript (what was heard in this session and what was done with it) is kept in memory only, never saved, and is cleared when Texprava closes. Voice commands are recognised by Texprava from the text the speech service returns.

If you download a Texprava speech pack, dictation in that language is recognised by Texprava itself, on your device. Sound from the microphone is held in memory only until each phrase has been recognised; it is never saved or sent anywhere.

Read aloud

Read aloud uses your phone's text-to-speech engine. Text is passed to that engine on your device; some voices offered by the engine may work online under the engine's own policy.

Scan text (photos and pictures)

Scan text reads printed text from a photo or picture you choose. A photo you take is captured by your phone's camera app and handed to Texprava through a one-time permission; a picture you choose comes through Android's picker, which gives Texprava only that picture. Texprava does not have permission to use the camera or to read your photo library. The text is recognised on your device by the Tesseract OCR engine and language models built into the app; the image is not sent anywhere. A photo taken for Scan text is kept only in the app's temporary storage until it has been read, then deleted. The recognised text is typed into your document and is handled like anything else you write.

PDFs and signatures

PDFs are opened, read, edited, signed and saved on your device; Texprava does not send them anywhere. A signature you draw is kept in the app's private storage so you can use it again. A digital signing certificate that Texprava makes for you is kept in the Android Keystore on your device; certificates you have installed are used through Android's certificate picker, which gives Texprava only the one you choose.

Downloads from Google Play and fonts

Speech packs and the font pack are optional downloads delivered by Google Play, as parts of the app, under Google Play's own terms; Texprava still has no internet access of its own. Fonts you add from your phone's storage are copied into the app's private storage and used only to show, print and save your documents and PDFs. A font may be embedded in a PDF you save, as PDFs normally carry their fonts.

Backups

Texprava turns off Android's automatic cloud backup of its private data. Your documents are backed up only if you save them to a location that you back up yourself, or if you make a Texprava backup.

A Texprava backup (Settings → Backup and restore) is made only when you ask for one. It contains your drafts, undo and version history, Word originals and app settings, encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256-GCM, with the key derived from your password), and it is saved only where you choose in Android's file picker. Texprava cannot recover a forgotten password. Locked notes stay encrypted with your device's key inside the backup and can be restored only on the device that locked them.

Children

Texprava is a general-purpose productivity app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds a feature that handles data differently (for example optional cloud sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
